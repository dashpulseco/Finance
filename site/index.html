What these numbers mean
  • Below all-time high - how far today's close sits under the highest close the proxy ETF has ever reached, as a percentage.
  • INVEST signal - the drawdown has reached the configured below the all-time high. That is the default; a fund with its own threshold says so on its card.
  • STRONG INVEST signal - the drawdown has reached the configured below the all-time high, again as the default for any fund that does not set its own.
  • 52w high - distance below the highest close of the last 52 weeks. It is listed as a supporting reason once it reaches the configured threshold.
  • 200-day SMA - today's close against the average of the last 200 closes.
  • 1 month and YTD - change since roughly a month ago and since the last close of last year.
  • Sparkline - drawdown from the all-time high over about the last three years, drawn from weekly closes. The two dashed lines are that card's own INVEST and STRONG INVEST thresholds, which are the defaults above unless the card says otherwise.

Rule-based reminder, not investment advice. Prices are closes of EUR-listed ETF proxies, not fund NAVs.
